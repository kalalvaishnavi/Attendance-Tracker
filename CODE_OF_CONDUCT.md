# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and
harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and considerate language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations
may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled with care.
